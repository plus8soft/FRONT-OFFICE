1.2Specify payment parameters

1st and 2nd digits: tax payment period: MC - month; KV - quarter; PL - half-year; GD - year.

3rd digit: period.

4th and 5th digits: period value: 01-12 for MC; 01-04 for KV; 01 or 02 for PL; 00 for GD.

6th digit: period.

7th to 10th digits: year for which tax is paid.

Example: MC.02.2017; KV.01.2017; PL.02.2017; GD.00.2017.

Specific date is entered in format: DD.MM.YYYY
