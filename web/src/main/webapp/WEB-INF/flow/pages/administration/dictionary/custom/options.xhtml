Scheduled update time is represented in the following format:

second(range 0-59); minute(range 0-59); hour(range 0-23); day of month(range 1-31); month(range 1-12); day of week(range 0-6, 0=Sunday).

The '*' symbol means - any value. The '?' symbol is used if a specific day of week(month) is not important.

Update frequency options:

"* * * * * *" = every second.

"0 0 * * * *" = every day at the start of every hour.

"0 0 8-10 * * *" = every day at 8, 9 and 10 o'clock.

"0 0/30 8-10 * * *" = every day at 8:00, 8:30, 9:00, 9:30 and 10 o'clock.

"0 0 9-17 * * MON-FRI" = every weekday from 9:00 to 17:00

"0 0 0 25 12 ?" = every December 25 at midnight

Time is specified in GMT+0, i.e. the launch will be performed at the specified hour + time zone. For example, for Moscow, if the "hour" value is "0", then the launch will be performed at 3:00.
