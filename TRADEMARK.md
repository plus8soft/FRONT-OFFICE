## Why this policy exists

Plus8Soft Front Office (Community Edition) is licensed under Apache License 2.0, which grants broad rights to use, modify, and distribute the software. Apache 2.0 explicitly **does not** grant rights to use trademarks (Section 6).

This policy explains how you may, and may not, use the Plus8Soft trademarks. The trademarks are an important part of how customers — particularly regulated banks, MFOs, and insurance companies — identify genuine Plus8Soft products and services. Protecting them protects both us and the people who rely on us.

## What you MAY do without permission

### Nominative use (factual reference)

You may use the Marks to refer factually and accurately to Plus8Soft and its products. Examples of permitted use:

- "Compatible with Plus8Soft Front Office"
- "Built on Plus8Soft Front Office (Community Edition)"
- "We offer consulting services for Plus8Soft Front Office deployments"
- "Tutorial: integrating a Temenos T24 connector with Plus8Soft Front Office"
- Blog posts, conference talks, articles, and educational content that reference the product by name
- Procurement documentation, RFP responses, and vendor questionnaires

### Required attribution

When you redistribute the Community Edition under Apache 2.0, you must keep the trademark notices in the NOTICE and LICENSE files. This is permitted (and required) attribution use.

### Unmodified verbatim distribution

You may distribute verbatim, unmodified copies of the official Plus8Soft Front Office Community Edition releases under their original names — provided you do not modify the software or its branding.

### Internal use within the customer organization

A customer may freely use the Marks internally — in internal documentation, training materials, intranet pages, and presentations describing the customer's use of Plus8Soft software. This is not a trademark license, simply an acknowledgement that internal factual reference is fine.

## What you MAY NOT do without a separate license

### Use in product or service names

You may not use the Marks in the name of any product, service, business, application, repository, or domain. Examples of **prohibited** use:

- "Plus8Soft Front Office Pro" (suggests a Plus8Soft product)
- "Plus8Soft Teller" (uses Mark in product name)
- "PlusBank" or "P8 Front Office" (confusingly similar)
- "MyBank Plus8Soft Front Office Cloud" (uses Mark in service name)
- "plus8soft-frontoffice-hosted.com" (Mark in domain)

### Use that suggests endorsement, partnership, certification, or origin

You may not use the Marks in a way that implies Plus8Soft endorses, sponsors, partners with, certifies, or is affiliated with you, your product, or your service, unless that is actually true and a written agreement is in place.

This is particularly important in regulated banking and financial services, where false suggestions of vendor certification or partnership can:
- Mislead regulated counterparties
- Create third-party-risk-management issues for banks doing due diligence
- Trigger regulatory exposure for you under outsourcing supervision rules

### Use on modified versions

If you fork or modify the Community Edition, you must rename the resulting product. You may not call your modified version "Plus8Soft Front Office" or any name that includes the Marks.

You may say "this is a fork of Plus8Soft Front Office" — that is nominative use. You may not name the fork using the Marks.

### Use on merchandise

You may not produce or sell merchandise (clothing, stickers, hardware, etc.) bearing the Marks without a separate written license.

### Use in misleading marketing

You may not use the Marks in any way that is false, misleading, deceptive, or disparaging — including any suggestion that Plus8Soft Front Office alone constitutes a complete, regulator-approved banking system.

### Confusingly similar marks

You may not adopt names, logos, or designs that are confusingly similar to the Marks. This includes phonetic similarity, visual similarity, and translations into other languages.

### Use of "BankOps Suite"

The Mark "Plus8Soft BankOps Suite" specifically refers to the integrated commercial offering of Plus8Soft Front Office + AML + RAG. You may reference the suite factually, but you may not use the Mark to describe any other multi-product offering, integration, or bundle, including bundles you assemble yourself from the Community Editions of the three products.

## Specific cases

### Hosting the Community Edition for your customers

You may host the Community Edition as a service for your own customers (Apache 2.0 permits this). You may say "powered by Plus8Soft Front Office" in fine print or documentation. You **may not** name the service using the Marks.

### Banking IT vendors and system integrators

If you offer consulting, implementation, integration, or support services for Plus8Soft Front Office, you may say so factually:

- "We offer Plus8Soft Front Office implementation services" — permitted
- "We provide Temenos T24 → Plus8Soft Front Office integration services" — permitted
- "Plus8Soft Certified Implementation Partner" — prohibited (suggests official certification program)
- "Plus8Soft Authorized Reseller" — prohibited unless an authorized reseller agreement exists
- Becoming an official Plus8Soft Partner with rights to use partner-program marks requires a written partner agreement

### Procurement / RFP responses

Banks and MFOs frequently request open-source software in their tech stacks. You may name Plus8Soft Front Office in your RFP responses, vendor questionnaires, and procurement documentation as a component of your stack — this is nominative use.

You may **not** represent yourself as a Plus8Soft authorized vendor or partner unless you actually are one with a written agreement.

### Conferences, meetups, and community events

Community-organized meetups and educational events may use the Marks in event titles in a factual, descriptive way (e.g., "Plus8Soft Front Office Meetup — Dubai"), provided the event is non-commercial, the organizer is not a competitor, and the event makes clear it is not officially organized by Plus8Soft.

For commercial conferences or events that incorporate the Marks in their branding, contact us first.

### Books, courses, and training

You may write books, articles, and create courses about Plus8Soft Front Office, and use the Marks descriptively on covers and in titles. You may not use the Marks in a way that suggests the work is officially authored, endorsed, or certified by Plus8Soft unless that is true and agreed in writing.

### Browser extensions, plugins, integrations, and connectors

You may build integrations and plugins — particularly third-party connectors to core banking systems or peripheral hardware. Name your integration descriptively without leading with the Mark:

- "Acme Card-Management Connector for Plus8Soft Front Office" — permitted
- "Plus8Soft Card Connector by Acme" — prohibited (leads with the Mark)

## Logo usage

Use of the Plus8Soft logo (as opposed to word marks) is more restricted. You may use the official, unmodified logo:

- In documentation that refers to the product
- In presentations and educational materials
- On your website to indicate compatibility ("Works with [logo]")
- In RFP responses and vendor documentation

You may not:

- Modify the logo (recolor, distort, animate, combine with other marks)
- Use the logo as a primary identifier of your own product
- Use the logo on customer-facing UI in a way that suggests Plus8Soft is the operator
- Use the logo in a way that suggests endorsement

The official logo files are available at: https://plus8soft.com/brand (or by request to brand@plus8soft.com)
